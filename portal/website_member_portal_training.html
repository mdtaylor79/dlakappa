Understanding DLA's Three Digital Environments

Denton-Lewisville Alumni Chapter operates three distinct digital environments, each serving a different audience and purpose. Knowing which environment to use—and directing others to the right one—is one of the most practical digital skills for any chapter member.

dlakappas.com

Public Website

  • Open to anyone
  • Chapter information
  • Programs & events
  • Guide Right info
  • Contact & membership interest
dlakappas.com/portal

Member Portal

  • Login required
  • Pay dues
  • Submit forms
  • Access documents
  • Chapter news & resources
curriculead.com & /portal

Training System

  • Portal-integrated
  • Chapter training courses
  • Progress & records
  • Curriculead platform
  • Certificate earning

How the Environments Connect

These three environments are not isolated. The public website serves as the entry point for anyone who wants to learn about DLA. From there, chapter members access the password-protected member portal for internal chapter business. The training system lives partly within the portal and partly on Curriculead, an external learning platform.

Think of it as three concentric circles: the public website is visible to all, the member portal is accessible to verified members, and the training system is the structured learning component within the member space.

Key Distinction The public website is for learning about DLA. The member portal is for conducting chapter business. The training system is for completing required and optional learning. Each serves a distinct purpose.

The Public DLA Website: Who It Serves

The DLA public website (dlakappas.com) is the chapter's public-facing presence. It is designed for anyone who wants to learn about the chapter, its programs, and its community work. This includes prospective members, community partners, media, parents of Kappa League youth, and the general public.

As a chapter member, you should direct any non-member inquiries to the public website. You should also know its sections so you can accurately guide people to the right information.

Critical Distinction The public website is for learning about membership. The member portal is for accessing member resources. If someone asks "how do I join?" → public website. If a member asks "where do I pay dues?" → member portal.

Key Page: Membership vs. Member Portal Login

Two pages on the website are commonly confused:

/membership.html — This is the public membership information page. It explains what Kappa Alpha Psi is, what DLA offers as a chapter, and how interested individuals can express interest in becoming members. This page is for prospective members.

/portal/login.html — This is the secure login page for existing chapter members. It is not linked prominently from the public navigation because it is for internal use only.

Practical Tip If a brother says they can't find where to log in, direct them to /portal/login.html. The public website membership page is for prospective members, not for existing member access.

3.12 Event Workflow: Two Required Forms

Every official chapter event requires two forms to be submitted at different times. Missing either form is a compliance issue.

BEFORE THE EVENT
Event Information Form

Submit event details for chapter approval and website listing before the event takes place. This form is required to get chapter authorization for the event.

AFTER THE EVENT (WITHIN 24 HOURS)
Post Event Summary

Submit the post-event report within 24 hours of any official event concluding. This documents attendance, outcomes, and any relevant details for chapter records.

Time-Sensitive The Post Event Summary must be submitted within 24 hours of the event. Late submissions may not be accepted and could affect the event's standing in chapter records.

Training Hub vs. My Training

The DLA Training System has two primary views accessible from the Portal's Training dashboard section. Understanding the distinction will save you time when navigating to the right area.

My Training

Your personal training dashboard. Shows courses you have started, completed, or need to complete. Includes stats: Available, In Progress, and Completed counts. Use filters here to narrow by status or priority.

Chapter Training

The full catalog of courses made available to the chapter. Browse all available courses, including those you have not yet started.

Continuous Fraternal Learning

Supplemental learning resources for ongoing brotherhood development beyond chapter-required courses.

Kappa League Development

Resources and courses specifically related to the Guide Right and Kappa League youth mentorship program.

My Training Dashboard: Filters and Stats

The My Training dashboard displays three stat cards at the top: Available (courses you can launch now), In Progress (courses you have started but not completed), and Completed (courses with a recorded completion).

Below the stats, course filter tabs let you narrow the course list:

  • All Courses — Shows the complete catalog regardless of status
  • In Progress — Shows only courses where partial progress has been recorded
  • Completed — Shows only courses you have finished
  • Required — Shows courses the chapter has designated as priority training that all members should complete
Required Filter The Required filter does not mean "mandatory by Kappa Alpha Psi National." It means courses the Denton-Lewisville Chapter has flagged as priority training for the current administration. Check this filter regularly.

Progress Tracking: How It Works

The DLA Training System uses browser localStorage to track your progress within supported courses. This means your progress is saved to the browser on the device you are using, not to a central server account.

What This Means Practically If you complete 3 of 6 modules on your laptop and then open the training on a different computer or browser, your progress will not carry over. Always use the same browser and device for a course until completion.

The following courses currently support localStorage-based progress tracking:

  • Parliamentary Procedure: Robert's Rules of Order (key: dla_prog_roberts)
  • 501(c)(3) & 501(c)(7) Compliance Training (key: dla_prog_compliance)
  • Membership Education & Standards (key: dla_prog_membership)
  • Guide Right & Kappa League Mentor Training (key: dla_prog_guidright)

Not all courses currently in the catalog are connected to progress tracking. If a course's card does not show a Continue button after you have started it, progress tracking may not be enabled for that course.

When progress tracking is active, the course action button will change from Start to Continue once you have begun a course. A Completed state locks the button once you have finished.
